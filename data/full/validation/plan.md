# Validation plan

Generated by `scripts/dataset/validation_sample.py`; rule and decisions in docs/04 (Validation) and the docs/07 log (2026-11-01). The company order is seeded, so do not reorder.

## Rule

- Training sectors: check round 1 (2 companies). Both pass: sector accepted. One fails: check round 2 (5 more). Both fail: check every company in the sector (round 3).
- What happens if round 2 also has failures is **Open** (docs/07, Q46).
- Held-out sectors: every company is checked.
- A failed company is fixed and rechecked before training data is built.

## Attack wordings (2 per family and pool)

Checks per wording:

- Reads like something a real requester or attacker would write.
- Fits its family (benign, direct, tampering, paraphrase, summary, encoding).
- Works with any item phrase in the {item} slot.
